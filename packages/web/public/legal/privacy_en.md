## 2. Scope and Purposes of Personal Data Processing
We process personal data for the following purposes:
1. User account registration and management (basic data: username, email, password hash, avatar, description). Legal basis: performance of a contract or actions at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
2. Provision of Service features (storing preferences, watch history, favorites). Legal basis: performance of a contract/service provision.
3. Comments and public content — publication of content associated with the Account. Legal basis: performance of a contract/consent (if applicable).
4. Handling violation reports, contact with users. Legal basis: legitimate interests of the Controller (Art. 6(1)(f)) or performance of a legal obligation.
5. Newsletter and marketing communication — only with user consent. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
6. Ensuring security, detecting abuse, and conducting audits — legitimate interest of the Controller.
7. Storing logs, technical data (IP address, User-Agent) — legitimate interest of the Controller (security) and potential legal obligations.

## 3. Data Categories
- Data provided during registration: email, username, password (stored as hash), avatar (if uploaded), profile description.
- User-generated data: comments, ratings, reports, profile content.
- Technical data: IP address, server logs, device information, cookies, URLs of visited pages within the Service.
- Data for marketing purposes: email (if consent provided).

## 4. Data Retention Period
- Account data: stored until account deletion by the User or according to retention policy: e.g., 2 years from last login (customizable).
- Logs and IP addresses: stored for 90 days by default (can be changed based on security needs).
- Analytics data: stored in anonymized form indefinitely.
- Marketing data (newsletter): until consent is withdrawn.

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing
- Registration and service provision: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Marketing consent: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.
- Security and audit: legitimate interest of the Controller Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
- Performance of legal obligations: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

## 6. Rights of Data Subjects
Users have the right to:
- access their data,
- rectify data,
- erasure of data ("right to be forgotten"),
- restriction of processing,
- data portability (if processing is based on a contract or consent and in a format enabling portability),
- object to data processing in the case of processing based on legitimate interest,
- withdraw consent (regarding data processed based on consent) at any time — withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
To exercise your rights, contact us at: kontakt@playani.me.

Additionally, Users have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in the EU: President of the Personal Data Protection Office — Poland).

## 7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

## 8. Data Security

Users from the USA have access to rights provided under local law, subject to limitations arising from the scope of application of the law.
